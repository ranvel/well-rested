Add proxy

Schemes: http://, https://, socks5:// and socks5h:// (SOCKS hostnames are resolved at the proxy; SOCKS needs a port). Credentials go in Username and Password, never in the URL; the password is kept in the Keychain. A CA bundle is trusted for the proxy and for every upstream routed through it (for proxies that re-sign TLS). Names can't be changed later: create a new proxy, repoint the upstreams, delete the old one.
